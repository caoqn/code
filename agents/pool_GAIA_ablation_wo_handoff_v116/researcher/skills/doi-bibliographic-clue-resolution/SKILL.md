---
name: doi-bibliographic-clue-resolution
description: Resolve a DOI-linked book and identify an author or person from textual clues with authoritative evidence
trigger: When a question provides a DOI plus a clue from a chapter or preview and asks for a person, author, or surname
---
1. Resolve the DOI through Crossref or the publisher platform, recording title, publication year, authors/editors, and stable URL.
2. Open the publisher or library record and inspect the table of contents to identify the relevant chapter and page range.
3. Search the exact clue phrase in quotation marks, prioritizing chapter PDFs, repository previews, and publisher full text; distinguish search snippets from directly fetched text.
4. Identify the person described by the clue using an authoritative biographical source (publisher, institutional profile, Britannica, or equivalent), rather than relying on an uncited snippet.
5. Confirm whether the requested output is the person’s name, the book author’s surname, or another linked entity; map the clue chain explicitly before answering.
6. Report the concise answer with URLs and quote the key passage(s), clearly labeling snippet evidence versus directly observed page content and any uncertainty.
