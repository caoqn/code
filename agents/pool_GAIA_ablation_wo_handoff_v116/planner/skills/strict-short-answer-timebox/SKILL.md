---
name: strict-short-answer-timebox
description: Efficient coordination workflow for research questions requiring a literal concise answer.
trigger: When a task asks for a strict short answer based on one or more external or attached sources.
---
1. Copy the complete question, answer-format rules, and any known clues into the first dispatch to the researcher and verifier.
2. Ask the researcher to identify the exact source chain and return one candidate answer with quoted evidence; ask the verifier to independently validate the candidate and wording.
3. Time-box source discovery: use targeted searches and stop once the source title and translation are directly corroborated.
4. Forward the evidence packet and candidate answer verbatim to the answer formatter, explicitly requiring the mandated prefix.
5. Check the formatter response literally for the required prefix and submit only the suffix, with no explanation or punctuation unless requested.
