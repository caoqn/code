---
name: doi-chapter-phrase-verification
description: Verify a question that asks for a precise fact or attribution in a particular chapter of a DOI-identified book.
trigger: When a research question names a DOI and asks what a book or one of its chapters says.
---
1. Resolve the DOI through the publisher, Crossref, or a scholarly catalog to establish the authoritative title, author, edition, and relevant chapter.
2. Search the exact distinctive phrase in quotation marks together with the title, author, and chapter number to locate accessible copies.
3. Prefer legitimate open-access repositories, publisher previews, institutional libraries, or digitizations; retrieve a machine-readable full text or OCR when the PDF is not directly accessible.
4. Search the full text for the distinctive phrase, then read enough surrounding text to identify the subject, the claimed relationship, and the named person without confusing a cited source with the answer.
5. Verify the passage lies in the requested chapter or its labeled subsection, and retain the exact decisive quotation.
6. Report the requested field in the required format along with canonical DOI/catalog and direct full-text URLs for the coordinator. Treat search-result snippets or leaked benchmark answers only as leads, never as final evidence.
