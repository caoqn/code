name: wikipedia-featured-article-history
description: Verify historical Wikipedia Featured Article promotion and nomination facts
trigger: When asked who nominated an article, especially within a date-bounded promotion list
---
1. Locate the relevant year/date promotion index and filter entries to the subject category described by the question.
2. Confirm uniqueness by checking all entries in the specified date section rather than relying on search snippets.
3. Open the article's Featured Article Candidate archive and distinguish the nominator field from the promoter or closing administrator.
4. Record the exact username spelling and verify it against an independent source or page version when practical.
5. Match the requested output role precisely: return the nominator, not the article title or promotion official, and obey the shortest-answer format.
